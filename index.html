ALCHEMY SORT

A dark-fantasy take on the liquid sort genre: pour glowing elixirs between glass flasks until every vessel holds one pure essence. Best played on a phone, with sound on.

PLAY

DESIGN NOTES

Why this genre
Sort puzzles are understood in three seconds, scale in difficulty with a single parameter (number of colours) and fit short mobile sessions.
The twist
Atmosphere instead of flat colour blocks: an alchemist's lab, candlelight, glass, glowing liquids, a sealing cork and a burst of sparks when a flask is pure. The goal is to make every pour feel satisfying.
Under the hood
Levels are generated procedurally and checked by a solver, so every level is guaranteed solvable. Difficulty grows from 3 to 7 essences. Undo and restart are built in; all visuals and sounds are code, no assets.
What I'd test next
A meta layer (each solved level brews an ingredient for a rare potion recipe book), “hidden” layers that reveal their colour only when on top, and whether a limited-undo economy with rewarded refills lifts session length and D1 retention.
